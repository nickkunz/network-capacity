# Supplementary Table 1. Corpus composition, substrate definitions, sources, and date provenance

Each row is one N3S. Nodes and edges describe the undirected substrate used for graph invariants. Events describe the observation stream aggregated to a daily series $r_t$. Observation windows are the native recording periods of each source, reported as start–end dates (or day indices where the source supplies only relative days) together with the span in days; windows were not standardized in duration, and each empirical maximum is conditional on its native window. Target reports the realized maximum daily event count $r_{\max}$ over that window and the estimation target $y^{*} = \ln(1 + r_{\max})$ from Eq. 4. Observation dates identify the records analyzed and are distinct from the dates on which a source or associated article was released or published. The final column records those source dates, versions, relative-date conventions, and fixed analysis cutoffs; a publication year outside the observation window is therefore expected rather than a discrepancy. Source citations refer to the Supplementary data-source references in [rf-citations.md](rf-citations.md).
